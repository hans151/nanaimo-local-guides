Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hans Suhr, REALTOR® (RE/MAX Anchor Realty) collects, uses, and protects personal information gathered through the Nanaimo Local Guides website.

What we collect

When you use the Weekend Planner and claim your plan, we collect: your first and last name, email address, mobile phone number, and the preferences you shared during the quiz (neighbourhood, group type, budget, vibes, dietary preferences). We also collect the date and time of submission.

How we use it

We use your contact information solely to: (1) send you the personalized Nanaimo weekend plan you requested, and (2) send occasional local tips and real estate market updates relevant to the Nanaimo and Central Vancouver Island area — but only if you checked the consent box at the time of submission.

Your information is stored in Hans Suhr's client relationship management system (CINC). It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

CASL compliance

We comply with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). You will only receive marketing messages if you provided express consent. Every message includes a working unsubscribe option.

PIPEDA compliance

We comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us.

Your rights

You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send, or by visiting nanaimo-local-guides.pages.dev/unsubscribe. To request access to or deletion of your data, email hans@hanssuhr.ca.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use third-party advertising trackers or sell data to ad networks. We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate site traffic.
